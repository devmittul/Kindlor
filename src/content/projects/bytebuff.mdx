# ByteBuff Case Study

ByteBuff is a custom-built, full-stack blogging platform designed for technical writers and developers to share their knowledge securely, build their personal brands, and reach new people across the internet.

## The Goal
1. Build a secure backend capable of handling user authentication and sessions.
2. Provide a decoupled, RESTful API architecture that delivers content rapidly to readers.
3. Create an ecosystem where authors can easily increase their online visibility and establish a digital identity.

## How Kindlor Built It
The backend is powered by **Node.js** and **Express**, utilizing **MongoDB** for flexible document storage. Security was a primary concern, so we implemented **JWT (JSON Web Tokens)** for stateless authentication, paired with role-based access control. 

Beyond just the technical architecture, ByteBuff was engineered to ensure that when an author hits "publish", their content is structured in a way that search engines love. By prioritizing speed and clean code, we gave writers the perfect platform to expand their audience, reach new people, and solidify their digital footprint on the web.
