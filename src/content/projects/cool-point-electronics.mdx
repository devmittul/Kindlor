# Cool Point Electronics Case Study

Cool Point Electronics required a digital transformation of their product catalog. Before our collaboration, they were heavily reliant on local foot traffic, lacking a professional online presence that could attract a wider audience.

## The Goal
1. Build a fast, scalable product catalog that establishes a strong digital identity.
2. Implement a robust database to track categories, pricing, and stock.
3. Heavily optimize the site for search engines so new people can discover their products online.

## How Kindlor Built It
We utilized **Astro** for static site generation, connecting it to a **Drizzle ORM** database layer. This ensures that every product page is pre-rendered for maximum speed and SEO efficiency—crucial for ranking high on Google and reaching new customers. 

By taking their vast physical inventory and giving it a structured, beautiful digital home, we provided Cool Point Electronics with a modern digital identity. The entire stack was deployed to **Cloudflare** via Wrangler, ensuring that no matter where a potential customer is searching from, the site loads instantly.
